Install on iPhone

Put it on your Home Screen

  1. Open this site in Safari.
  2. Tap the Share button.
  3. Choose Add to Home Screen.
  4. Leave Open as Web App enabled, then tap Add.

After installation it opens in its own app window. The current volume remains readable offline after the first successful load.

Growing collection

New content without reinstalling

Future volumes can be published to the same address. The installed app checks the catalogue whenever it opens online. New volumes then appear in this library, while previously opened material remains available offline.
